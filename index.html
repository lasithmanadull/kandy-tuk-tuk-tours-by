About Us

Welcome to Kandy Tuk Tuk Tours by Munasingha

Your Local Friend in Kandy 🌿

"Experience the beauty of Kandy with a friendly local tuk-tuk driver. Discover hidden places, amazing views, cultural attractions, and unforgettable experiences. Enjoy a comfortable and personalized journey around Kandy."

From the sacred Temple of the Tooth and the calm waters of Kandy Lake to misty tea plantations and hidden waterfalls — Munasingha knows every beautiful corner of the hill capital. No crowded buses, no rigid schedules. Just you, the breeze, and the real Kandy.

  • ✔ Hotel pick-up & drop-off anywhere in Kandy
  • ✔ Custom routes built around your interests
  • ✔ Honest local prices — no hidden fees
